Craft beer and the football on, in the middle of SCBD — and something back for every FPL Jakarta member who turns up on a Premier League matchday.

01  What members get
FPL Jakarta members
Every league member is eligible for discounts and benefits here during Premier League games, all season long.
Mention FPL Jakarta when you order.

Taproom 22 is a craft beer bar built around sports screenings — a rotating tap list of local Indonesian brewers alongside imports, screens running through the day, and food to go with it.

Which makes it the obvious place to be when the matches that decide your gameweek are on. Bring the live table up on your phone, watch your captain blank in company, and settle it over a pint rather than in the group chat.
