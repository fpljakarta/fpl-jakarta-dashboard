Milo's Padel

Six courts in Kemang with the football on the screens — and something back for every FPL Jakarta member who plays on a Premier League matchday.

01  What members get
FPL Jakarta members
Every league member is eligible for discounts and benefits here during Premier League games, all season long.
Mention FPL Jakarta when you book or arrive.

Six double courts in Kemang, open from early until late, with the football showing alongside — so a Saturday can be a game and a gameweek rather than a choice between them.

Courts book through their own app, which is worth having if you play more than once. Between games, the live table will tell you exactly how much damage your captain has done.
