FPL Jakarta × Partner venue

Milo's Padel

Six courts in Kemang, the football on the screens, and the only place in the association where a bad gameweek can be worked off rather than merely regretted.

01  Why we're here

Milo's Padel is the other venue FPL Jakarta is associated with, and the one where you get to move. Six double courts in Kemang, open from early until late, with the football showing alongside — so a Saturday can be a game and a gameweek rather than a choice between them.

Courts book through their own app, which is worth having if you play more than once. Between games, the live table will tell you exactly how much damage your captain has done.
