Taproom 22

Our home in SCBD. Craft beer, the football on, and enough of the league in one room that somebody will always tell you your captain was a mistake.

01  Why we're here

Taproom 22 is one of two venues FPL Jakarta is associated with, and the one in the middle of the business district. It is a craft beer bar built around sports screenings — a rotating tap list of local Indonesian brewers alongside imports, screens running through the day, and food to go with it.

Which makes it the obvious place to be when the matches that decide your gameweek are on. Bring the live table up on your phone, watch your captain blank in company, and settle it over a pint rather than in the group chat.
