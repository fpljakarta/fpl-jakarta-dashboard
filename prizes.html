When it's paid

Every prize on this page is paid out at the end of the 2026/27 season. That includes the ones decided long before then — a Manager of the Month won in October and a Top Gun won in GW3 are both settled when the season is.

Nothing is paid mid-season, so a trophy on the awards page is a record of what you have earned, not of what has already landed.

If it ends level

Tied placings pool the prizes for the places they occupy and split them equally.

A single prize that ties is split between everyone level on points. Two managers tied for Manager of the Month on take each. The same goes for Top Gun and for highest team value.

Prize money is set by the FPL Jakarta organisers, not by this site, and this page is a record of it rather than the authority on it. Placings are taken from the final official Fantasy Premier League standings for each league once the season is complete.
