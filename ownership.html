Every footballer somebody in FPL Jakarta is fielding, ranked by how heavily the league backs him — and set against how heavily the rest of the world does. Tap a player to see exactly who owns him.

01  The board
League EO
Share of the league fielding him, with a captaincy counted twice. Above 100% is normal.
Global
Selected by this share of every FPL manager in the world, as FPL reports it.
Edge
League EO minus global. A big positive means the league is far heavier on him than the world is.

Effective ownership is measured inside FPL Jakarta, not globally. Bench players do not count towards it unless their manager has played a Bench Boost, which is the same rule the live table uses.
